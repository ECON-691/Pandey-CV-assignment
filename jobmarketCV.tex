\subsection*{Research in Progress}

{\bf Pandey, S.}, Work choice optimization of a informal labour facing urban-flooding using Dynamic Programming.

Abstract: A representative informal daily wage worker faces the challenge of urban flooding that reduces his productivity due to negative impact on his health and standard of living. Moreover, due to lack of job protection, he/she cannot stop working. With the rising probability of urban flooding, not just during monsoons, but round the year due climate change, he/she faces rising risk of job loss. However, lately, to mitigate the risk of urban flooding, the government has started investing in Nature-Based solutions (NBS), which act as a flood buffer. However, work in NBS pays less than what regualar work does. Each week he/she chooses a time allocation between regular work and NBS work. Hence, I am trying to optimize the informal labour's allocation of their working time between regular work and NBS work over a year, considering NBS work pays less but helps reduce flood damage.  \\
 
Chaudhary, V., {\bf Pandey, S.}, Goswami, A., Impact of ESG performance on AI adoption: Case Study of MSMEs Through an Econometric Modelling Approach.

Abstract: This paper deals with nature, degree and the kind of the impact of Environment (E), Social (S), and Governance (G) performance of 21460 large and MSME firms of Faridabad district of Haryana, India on their artificial intelligence (AI) adoption at a sub-national level. The research investigates the causal connection using two-stage least squares (2SLS) instrumental variables (IV) estimation model. The model dataset had a challenge of missing data in two key variables, social performance and AI adoption. This was solved using multiple imputation by chained equations (MICE). The IV/2SLS estimation method was performed instrumenting governance performance with industry category (manufacturing/services) and plot size of firm as IVs. The tests also showed that the IVs, were strong and valid. 2SLS estimates suggested that only social performance and nature/structure/type of the firms were significant in impacting AI adoption. \\
